# 09. Quantum Entropic Dissociation

## Error Correction and Damage Budget Lab

### Original vision

Keep crucial biology in superposition so it bypasses thermodynamic decay.

### Evidence boundary

Quantum coherence does not imply thermodynamic exemption, genetic repair, or human immortality. The included code uses classical bits only.

### Buildable proposal

Demonstrate three-copy majority decoding under controlled bit flips and record redundancy and correction limits.

### Testable hypothesis

A visible failure envelope helps distinguish corrected single-bit errors from misleading claims of universal recovery.

### Baseline comparison

One unencoded bit with identical injected fault scenarios.

### Measurements

Logical error rate, physical error rate, redundancy overhead, and failure under correlated faults.

### Next implementation steps

Enumerate single and multiple errors; add probabilistic independent/correlated-noise experiments; use a vetted quantum simulator only for a separately specified quantum experiment.

### Failure modes

Confusing quantum and classical correction, correlated failures, unmeasured correction costs, and biology extrapolation.

### Sponsor or collaborator fit

Error-correction educator, reliability lab, or open-source simulator maintainer; fund a reproducible fault-model benchmark.

### Runnable source

`labs.core.repetition_decode` in `labs/core.py`. Run all examples with `python -m labs.demo`; inspect `tests/test_labs.py`.

### Six-week milestone proposal

Weeks 1–2: specify data and assumptions with an independent reviewer. Weeks 3–4: extend the demonstrator and run a preregistered baseline comparison. Weeks 5–6: publish reproducible results, failure cases, and a go/no-go recommendation. This schedule is a planning estimate, not a commitment or evidence of feasibility beyond the bounded software task.

Success means a reproducible result and honest limitations, including a null or negative result. No lab, clinical, animal, radiation, or environmental intervention is authorized by this proposal.
