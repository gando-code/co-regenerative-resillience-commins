# 10. Co-Transcendent Hyper-Flora Architecture

## Human–Habitat Recovery Twin

### Original vision

Merge consciousness and biology with a sentient planetary biosphere to achieve immortality.

### Evidence boundary

Ecological interdependence does not establish planetary sentience, consciousness merging, or individual immortality.

### Buildable proposal

Track bounded transfers among named resource pools and compare restoration interventions in a transparent ecological model.

### Testable hypothesis

Explicit conservation and unmet needs make ecosystem proposals more auditable than an unconstrained benefit score.

### Baseline comparison

Independent silo budgets with no shared resource accounting.

### Measurements

Resource conservation, shortage duration, calibration error, intervention cost, and habitat-specific outcomes chosen by field experts.

### Next implementation steps

Add external inputs/losses and multiple stocks; calibrate to permitted published data; recruit an ecologist and community steward before field collection.

### Failure modes

Oversimplified ecology, model misuse, sensitive-species location exposure, and decisions without local consent.

### Sponsor or collaborator fit

Conservation research group or Conservation X Labs matching challenge; seek a bounded monitoring/recovery pilot with expert-led evaluation.

### Runnable source

`labs.core.ecosystem_step` in `labs/core.py`. Run all examples with `python -m labs.demo`; inspect `tests/test_labs.py`.

### Six-week milestone proposal

Weeks 1–2: specify data and assumptions with an independent reviewer. Weeks 3–4: extend the demonstrator and run a preregistered baseline comparison. Weeks 5–6: publish reproducible results, failure cases, and a go/no-go recommendation. This schedule is a planning estimate, not a commitment or evidence of feasibility beyond the bounded software task.

Success means a reproducible result and honest limitations, including a null or negative result. No lab, clinical, animal, radiation, or environmental intervention is authorized by this proposal.

### Commercial/public-interest structure

Keep the reusable evaluation core open under MIT; negotiate payment for specific engineering, integration, or review milestones. No automatic royalty is implied. A real sponsor budget requires scoped labor and any institution-specific approvals. See `docs/STRATEGY.md` for professional contact routes and funding restrictions.
