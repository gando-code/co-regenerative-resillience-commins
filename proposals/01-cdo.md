# 01. Chrono-Dynamic Osmosis

## Historical State and Recovery Atlas

### Original vision

Consciously diffuse earlier biological states into the present.

### Evidence boundary

No demonstrated method for shifting a body backward through experiential time is supplied. A past measurement is a reference, not a recoverable biological template.

### Buildable proposal

Compare repeated, normalized measurements to a declared historical baseline; retain dates, units, missingness, and provenance. Use synthetic system-health measurements first.

### Testable hypothesis

An explicitly historical baseline makes deviations easier to audit than an unlabelled current-score dashboard.

### Baseline comparison

Unlabelled dashboard and fixed-threshold alerts.

### Measurements

Measurement completeness, reference-selection errors, and blinded reviewer agreement; never infer years of rejuvenation.

### Next implementation steps

Add measurement schemas and normalization; compare historical and rolling baselines on synthetic drift; recruit a measurement-science reviewer.

### Failure modes

Unit mismatch, cherry-picked baselines, regression to the mean, and confusing clock changes with functional recovery.

### Sponsor or collaborator fit

Research data tooling grant or paid quality-assurance pilot; collaborate with an aging-data laboratory only after it defines a suitable nonclinical evaluation.

### Runnable source

`labs.core.historical_distance` in `labs/core.py`. Run all examples with `python -m labs.demo`; inspect `tests/test_labs.py`.

### Six-week milestone proposal

Weeks 1–2: specify data and assumptions with an independent reviewer. Weeks 3–4: extend the demonstrator and run a preregistered baseline comparison. Weeks 5–6: publish reproducible results, failure cases, and a go/no-go recommendation. This schedule is a planning estimate, not a commitment or evidence of feasibility beyond the bounded software task.

Success means a reproducible result and honest limitations, including a null or negative result. No lab, clinical, animal, radiation, or environmental intervention is authorized by this proposal.

### Commercial/public-interest structure

Keep the reusable evaluation core open under MIT; negotiate payment for specific engineering, integration, or review milestones. No automatic royalty is implied. A real sponsor budget requires scoped labor and any institution-specific approvals. See `docs/STRATEGY.md` for professional contact routes and funding restrictions.
