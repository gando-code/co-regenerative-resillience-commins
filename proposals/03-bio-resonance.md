# 03. Axiom-Breaking Bio-Resonance

## Bounded Energy and Repair Budget Explorer

### Original vision

Tissue rewrites foundational laws and fuels indefinite regeneration from ambient disorder.

### Evidence boundary

Adaptation can change regulation, not repeal conservation laws. Ambient energy needs a usable gradient, conversion mechanism, and measured losses.

### Buildable proposal

Account for harvested energy, stored reserves, load, and deficits in a finite time interval. Use a virtual sensor node, not living tissue.

### Testable hypothesis

Explicit deficit accounting identifies configurations that cannot sustain an intended monitoring interval.

### Baseline comparison

Model that omits reserve depletion or assumes a constant power source.

### Measurements

Energy balance in joules, operating hours before depletion, conversion losses, and forecast error on a later measured device.

### Next implementation steps

Add watts-to-joules integration and efficiency; compare with a known load; instrument a low-power nonmedical sensor only after a reviewed electrical design.

### Failure modes

Confusing heat with freely extractable work, incorrect units, and extrapolating device results to human metabolism.

### Sponsor or collaborator fit

Energy-harvesting hardware company or university power-electronics collaborator; request measured traces and review first.

### Runnable source

`labs.core.energy_budget` in `labs/core.py`. Run all examples with `python -m labs.demo`; inspect `tests/test_labs.py`.

### Six-week milestone proposal

Weeks 1–2: specify data and assumptions with an independent reviewer. Weeks 3–4: extend the demonstrator and run a preregistered baseline comparison. Weeks 5–6: publish reproducible results, failure cases, and a go/no-go recommendation. This schedule is a planning estimate, not a commitment or evidence of feasibility beyond the bounded software task.

Success means a reproducible result and honest limitations, including a null or negative result. No lab, clinical, animal, radiation, or environmental intervention is authorized by this proposal.

### Commercial/public-interest structure

Keep the reusable evaluation core open under MIT; negotiate payment for specific engineering, integration, or review milestones. No automatic royalty is implied. A real sponsor budget requires scoped labor and any institution-specific approvals. See `docs/STRATEGY.md` for professional contact routes and funding restrictions.
