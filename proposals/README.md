## Evidence and reading

Primary work provides narrow precedents, not support for the extraordinary mechanisms.

- Kwak et al., *Mosaic partial epidermal reprogramming remodels…*, Nature Communications (2026), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69047-2 . Identified as related partial-reprogramming research; full text could not be retrieved in this session. No clinical or quantitative claim is derived from its snippet.
- Bonnet et al., *Bayesian continual learning and forgetting in neural networks* (2025), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64601-w . Related learning research; full text could not be retrieved. Not evidence for limitless cognition or identity continuity.
- Ni et al., *Soft shape-programmable surfaces by fast electromagnetic actuation of liquid metal networks*, Nature Communications 13, 5576 (2022), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-31092-y . Retrieved primary report of engineered morphing surfaces. Does not demonstrate biological phase shifting.
- van de Ven, Soures, Kudithipudi, *Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting* (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05175 . Author-written overview, not a biological-immortality finding.
- IBM quantum communication explanation: https://www.ibm.com/quantum/blog/networked-quantum-computers . Entanglement does not permit instantaneous messaging.

Check original papers, data access, and reproduction rights before adding quantitative experiments. Review biological hypotheses with qualified researchers; this repository contains no experimental biological protocols. Research checked October 23, 2026.
